\title{Circularly Polarized Radio Emission from an M6--M7 Dwarf in a RACS Census of Gaia Ultracool Dwarfs}

\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
We measured Stokes~$V$ at the positions of \ucdvRealNCensus\ ultracool dwarfs from the
\citet{reyle2018} Gaia DR2 sample within \ucdvRealDmax~pc, in the RACS-low2 (887.5~MHz) and
RACS-low3 (943.5~MHz) epochs of the Rapid ASKAP Continuum Survey. The median $3\sigma$ limit on
$|V|$ is \ucdvRealLimit~mJy~beam$^{-1}$, and at most a fraction \ucdvRealFracFifteen\ of the
snapshots sensitive to $L_{\nu,V}=10^{15}$~erg~s$^{-1}$~Hz$^{-1}$ exceed it. One dwarf,
2MASS~J11231964$-$0509045, an M6--M7 flare star at \ucdvRealDetDist~pc, shows
$V=+\ucdvRealDetV\pm\ucdvRealDetSigmaV$~mJy~beam$^{-1}$ in RACS-low3 with $|V|/I\approx
\ucdvRealDetFrac$, and a deeper EMU observation in \ucdvRealFuBestDate\ shows $V=+\ucdvRealFuBestV
\pm\ucdvRealFuBestSigmaV$~mJy~beam$^{-1}$ with the same sign. It is not listed in the ASKAP
radio-star or circular-polarization catalogues we checked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Sample and measurement}
\label{sec:method}
Ultracool-dwarf radio emission is coherent and often strongly circularly polarized, so a
Stokes~$V$ survey selects it against the unpolarized extragalactic background. The blind
VLASS search of the \citet{reyle2018} candidates at 3~GHz used Stokes~$I$ alone
\citep{perger2025}, and the RACS-low2 circular-polarization catalogue measures $V$ only at
Stokes~$I$ catalogue positions \citep{duchesne2026}. We instead measure $V$ at every dwarf.

The parent sample is the \citet{reyle2018} table of Gaia DR2 \citep{gaiadr2} candidates.
Of its \ucdvRealNReyle\ entries, \ucdvRealNBoth\ fall inside both a low2 and a low3 Stokes~$V$
image on CASDA; RACS-low1 has no $V$ products there. The fetch rate measured on
\ucdvRealSzeroN\ test pairs (\ucdvRealSzeroOk\ succeeded, \ucdvRealSzeroSec~s per pair)
projected \ucdvRealFullHours~h for the full sample; a rule fixed beforehand then set a distance
limit of \ucdvRealDmax~pc, chosen from parallax alone. For each dwarf and epoch we retrieved a
$0.06^\circ$ $I$ and $V$ cutout from one observation \citep{mcconnell2020} with
\code{astroquery} \citep{astroquery}, moved the Gaia position to the observation date with its
proper motion, and read $I$ and $V$ at the pixel containing it. The noise is the standard
deviation in an annulus of 30--90\arcsec.

A target-epoch counts as a candidate if $|V|/\sigma_V\geq5$, if $|V|/I$ exceeds the local
leakage floor when Stokes~$I$ is itself detected, and if no Stokes~$I$ source brighter than
100~mJy lies within 3\arcmin. A slot whose cutout has too few bright sources to set the floor is
held for inspection. The upper limit on $|V|$ is $|V|+3\sigma_V$.

\section{Controls}
\label{sec:controls}
The thresholds and pass criteria were fixed before any target value was examined. The checks
used the \ucdvRealSzeroN\ test pairs, whose dwarfs come from the half of the sample (split by a
hash of the Gaia identifier) that sets no threshold. Forced photometry at
\ucdvRealConeN\ blank positions 2\arcmin\ from the targets gave positive $V$ in a fraction
\ucdvRealConePos\ of cases with $\mathrm{std}(V/\sigma_V)=\ucdvRealConeStd$, as expected
for noise; a peak search instead of a fixed pixel would bias this fraction towards one. Point
sources injected into the real cutouts (\ucdvRealCtwoN\ per level) were recovered at a rate of
\ucdvRealCtwoComp\ at $7\sigma_V$, with a median recovered-to-injected flux ratio of
\ucdvRealCtwoRatio\ above $10\sigma_V$. For the \ucdvRealCthreeN\ stars of the RACS-low2
circular-polarization catalogue \citep{duchesne2026}, measured in their own observations, the
median ratio of our $V$ to the catalogue value is \ucdvRealCthreeRatio, with
\ucdvRealCthreeFlips\ sign reversals. The T8 dwarf WISE~J0623$-$0456, a known periodic emitter
outside the Gaia sample \citep{rose2023}, gives $V=\ucdvRealCthreebLowtwoV$ and
$\ucdvRealCthreebLowthreeV$~mJy~beam$^{-1}$ ($V/\sigma_V=\ucdvRealCthreebLowtwoSnr$ and
$\ucdvRealCthreebLowthreeSnr$) in low2 and low3.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
Of the census slots, \ucdvRealNMissing\ low3 images were refused by the archive and
\ucdvRealNInsane\ cutouts failed the noise check, leaving \ucdvRealNSane\ of \ucdvRealNSlots\
target-epochs with limits; the median $\sigma_V$ is \ucdvRealSigmaV~mJy~beam$^{-1}$. The
\ucdvRealNullN\ blank-sky measurements made alongside them give positive $V$ in a fraction
\ucdvRealNullPos\ with $\mathrm{std}(V/\sigma_V)=\ucdvRealNullStd$. No dwarf changes between the
two epochs beyond the allowance for a 10\% flux-scale error and the band difference
(\ucdvRealNPairs\ pairs). A snapshot is counted as sensitive to $L_{\nu,V}$ if $5\sigma_V$ at
the dwarf's distance reaches it; \ucdvRealNeffFifteen\ and \ucdvRealNeffSixteen\ snapshots are
sensitive to $10^{15}$ and $10^{16}$~erg~s$^{-1}$~Hz$^{-1}$, and the 95\% limits on the fraction
above those luminosities are \ucdvRealFracFifteen\ and \ucdvRealFracSixteen\
(Figure~\ref{fig:census}a).

One slot reached $5\sigma$ and was held, its cutout having too few bright sources to set a
leakage floor. It is 2MASS~J11231964$-$0509045 (Gaia DR2 \ucdvRealDetGaia) in RACS-low3
observation \ucdvRealDetObs\ on \ucdvRealDetDate: $V=+\ucdvRealDetV\pm\ucdvRealDetSigmaV$ and
$I=\ucdvRealDetI$~mJy~beam$^{-1}$ ($I/\sigma_I=\ucdvRealDetIsnr$), a compact source at the
propagated position, with $|V|/I=\ucdvRealDetFrac\pm\ucdvRealDetFracErr$, far above
instrumental leakage. In low2 it gives $V/\sigma_V=\ucdvRealDetOtherSnr$. The Stokes~$I$
source is catalogued, unidentified, by \citet{thomson2026}. At $L_{\nu,V}\approx\ucdvRealDetL$
~erg~s$^{-1}$~Hz$^{-1}$ it lies below the luminosities quoted above. The star has spectral type
M6V from a near-infrared spectrum \citep{davoudi2025} and M7 from photometry \citep{reyle2018},
and a rotation period of 0.129~d \citep{newton2018}.

After the census we measured the star in every ASKAP observation of its position with $I$ and
$V$ images (\ucdvRealFuNObs\ observations, \ucdvRealFuNRefused\ refused, \ucdvRealFuNMeas\
measured, \ucdvRealFuFirstYear--\ucdvRealFuLastYear; VAST \citealt{murphy2021}, EMU
\citealt{hopkins2025}; Figure~\ref{fig:census}b). EMU observation \ucdvRealFuBestObs\ gives
$V=+\ucdvRealFuBestV\pm\ucdvRealFuBestSigmaV$~mJy~beam$^{-1}$ ($V/\sigma_V=\ucdvRealFuBestSnr$,
$I/\sigma_I=\ucdvRealFuBestIsnr$), with a chance probability of $\ucdvRealFuBestP$, or
$\ucdvRealFuBestPTrials$ allowing for the \ucdvRealFuNIndep\ observations searched. Of these,
\ucdvRealFuNPos\ reach $2.5\sigma$, all with positive $V$, and \ucdvRealFuNNeg\ fall below
$-2.5\sigma$; the remaining \ucdvRealFuSubN\ average $V/\sigma_V=\ucdvRealFuSubMean$, so the
emission is not steady at a lower level. This follow-up was not part of the fixed census.

The star is absent from the ASKAP radio-star and circular-polarization catalogues of
\citet{pritchard2021}, \citet{pritchard2024}, \citet{driessen2024} and \citet{duchesne2026}.
Intermittent, strongly circularly polarized emission of one sign from a rapidly rotating late-M
dwarf is the behaviour of electron-cyclotron maser emission \citep{hallinan2008}; the snapshots
do not resolve its time structure, which would test that interpretation.
