\title{Environment-Split HI Mass Functions in FASHI DR2 Against Two Null Tests}
\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
We split the HI mass function of FASHI DR2 by environment inside the SDSS footprint, where voids
and groups are catalogued, and compare the knee offsets with two nulls: void- and group-shaped
volumes placed at random, and environment labels shuffled among galaxies at fixed redshift and
survey depth with the real number of members. With each galaxy's $V_{\rm max}$ restricted to its
environment, the void$-$wall offset of $\feRealEnvVoidOffset$~dex exceeds the two nulls by
\feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma\ and \feRealShufVoidEnvSigma$\sigma$, and the group$-$field offset of
$+\feRealEnvGroupOffset$~dex by \feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma\ and \feRealShufGroupEnvSigma$\sigma$. The
group excess lies in members with a neighbour of the same group inside the FAST beam; without them
the offset is $+\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvClean$~dex. Their line-width excess grows with the
neighbour search radius, which beam blending does not predict. We claim neither offset as
environmental.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Void galaxies are expected to have a lower HI mass function (HIMF) knee than wall galaxies, and
group galaxies to lose gas to stripping \citep{solanes2001,jones2018}; \citet{moorman2014}, with an
estimator that does not assume uniform density, found the ALFALFA void knee $\sim$0.14~dex lower.
The FAST All Sky HI Survey \citep[FASHI;][]{fashi_dr1,fashi_dr2} now provides \feRealNDRTwo\
sources with a completeness $C$ and accessible volume $V_{\rm max}$ for each; the one environment
study on FASHI data used 230 group galaxies \citep{fashi_groups}. Environment splits of a
flux-limited $1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMF also select in distance, depth and structure, so we compare them
with two nulls that fail in different ways, and test blending in FAST's beam. An earlier version
of this analysis, on DR1 with a single flux-limit weighting, reported a void offset of
$\feRealDROneMaxVoidKneeOffset$~dex without a null test.

\section{Data and Method}
\label{sec:data}
We keep the sources of Table~2 of \citet{fashi_dr2} with $C\geq\feRealCMin$, the sample of the
survey's own HIMF; weights $1/(C_i V_i)$ reproduce the published global HIMF
($\log M^*=\feRealGlobalLogMStar$, $\alpha=\feRealGlobalAlpha$, against 9.89 and $-1.31$).
Schechter fits are formally poor (reduced $\chi^2$ up to \feRealGlobalRedChi); their errors are
scaled accordingly. We use the \feRealBlendNPool\ sources inside both a padded box around the
Douglass+2023 VoidFinder voids \citep{douglass2023} and the SDSS footprint (1$^\circ$ cells
holding Tempel+2017 galaxies). Void members lie inside any void hole and walls are the rest; group
members lie within the projected $R_{200}$ and 1000~km\,s$^{-1}$ of a Tempel+2017 group
\citep{tempel2017}. Besides the survey-wide weighting we weight each galaxy by
$C_i V_i\,g_E(d_{\max,i})$, where $g_E(D)$ is the share of the volume within $D$ occupied by its
environment $E$, from $2\times10^6$ randoms classified by the same rules.

The first null moves each void rigidly to a random position with all its holes in the footprint,
and each group to a random position with its velocity and $R_{200}$. The second gives the
environment label to the real number of members in each cell of redshift ($\Delta z=0.0025$) and
survey depth (terciles of the catalogue rms, split at \feRealRmsEdgeLo\ and \feRealRmsEdgeHi\
mJy), drawn at random (\feRealShufReps\ shuffles). A source is flagged as possibly blended when two
or more SDSS galaxies ($r<17.77$), one its counterpart, lie within one FAST beam (2.9~arcmin) and
$W_{50}/2+100$~km\,s$^{-1}$ of it. Each excess over a null is divided by the measured offset's fit
error and the null spread in quadrature.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
The measured offsets are $\feRealVoidKneeOffset\pm\feRealVoidKneeErr$ (void) and
$+\feRealGroupKneeOffset\pm\feRealGroupKneeErr$~dex (group) with the survey-wide weighting, and
$\feRealEnvVoidOffset\pm\feRealEnvVoidErr$ and $+\feRealEnvGroupOffset\pm\feRealEnvGroupErr$~dex
with the restricted one. Against the placements the void excess is $\feRealNullCExcess$ and
$\feRealEnvNullExcess$~dex (\feRealNullCExcessSigmaQuad\ and \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma$\sigma$) and the
group excess $+\feRealGroupNullExcess$ and $+\feRealEnvGroupNullExcess$~dex
(\feRealGroupNullExcessSigmaQuad\ and \feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma$\sigma$). Against the shuffles they
are $\feRealShufVoidSurveyExcess$ and $\feRealShufVoidEnvExcess$~dex (\feRealShufVoidSurveySigma\
and \feRealShufVoidEnvSigma$\sigma$) and $+\feRealShufGroupSurveyExcess$ and
$+\feRealShufGroupEnvExcess$~dex (\feRealShufGroupSurveySigma\ and \feRealShufGroupEnvSigma$\sigma$);
shuffling on redshift alone gives larger excesses ($\feRealShufZVoidEnvExcess$ and
$+\feRealShufZGroupEnvExcess$~dex restricted).

The two nulls hold different things fixed. The random void placements hold
\feRealNullCNInVoidMin--\feRealNullCNInVoidMax\ galaxies against \feRealNInVoid\ in the real voids,
and their offsets correlate weakly with occupancy (correlation $\feRealEnvNullCorrOccEnv$
restricted, $\feRealEnvNullCorrOccSurvey$ survey-wide), emptier placements giving less negative
offsets; the placed voids also contain real void members at \feRealNullCOverlap\%, against
\feRealVoidBaseRatePct\% of the sample. Both effects make the placement excess the conservative
end. The shuffles match occupancy, redshift and depth but not spatial coherence.

Of the group members, \feRealBlendGroupWfiftyFlagIn\% are flagged, against
\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyFlagOut\% of field galaxies, and for \feRealBlendGroupSameGroupPct\% of the
flagged members the neighbour belongs to the same group, so the flag largely marks compact group
cores. Without flagged sources the restricted group offset is
$+\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvClean\pm\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvCleanErr$~dex
(\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyShufSigma$\sigma$ over the shuffles) and the void offset
$\feRealBlendVoidWfiftyEnvClean$~dex (\feRealBlendVoidWfiftyShufSigma$\sigma$). At fixed HI mass and
redshift, flagged group members have $\log W_{50}$ larger by
$\feRealBlendGroupLwInDiff\pm\feRealBlendGroupLwInSe$~dex; the excess is \feRealBlendGroupLwRsmallDiff,
\feRealBlendGroupLwInDiff\ and \feRealBlendGroupLwRlargeDiff~dex for search radii of 1.5, 2.9 and
4.5~arcmin.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
The void excess is \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma--\feRealShufVoidEnvSigma$\sigma$ depending on the null,
and we do not report it as a detection. The two nulls cannot be reconciled directly: a void is
emptier than a typical randomly placed volume, so no rigid placement matches its occupancy, and the
shuffle that does match it has no spatial structure.

The group excess is larger, \feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma--\feRealShufGroupEnvSigma$\sigma$ with the
restricted weighting, and it sits in compact group cores. Blending of neighbours in the beam would
raise the HI mass there, but a neighbour outside the beam cannot broaden the profile, and the
line-width excess grows with search radius instead of falling; broader lines at fixed HI mass are
also expected of gas-poor galaxies in dense cores. The present data do not separate the two, so we
do not attribute the group offset to environment; interferometric HI for flagged members would.
For environment-split $1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMFs from single-dish surveys we recommend both nulls,
conditioned on redshift and depth and reported with their occupancy, and a confusion test that
holds mass fixed and varies the search radius.
