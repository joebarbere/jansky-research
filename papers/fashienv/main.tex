\title{Environment-Split HI Mass Functions in FASHI DR2 Against Two Null Tests}
\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
We split the HI mass function of FASHI DR2 by environment inside the SDSS footprint, where voids
and groups are catalogued, and compare the knee offsets with two nulls: void- and group-shaped
volumes placed at random, and environment labels shuffled among galaxies at fixed redshift and
detection sensitivity with the real number of members. With each galaxy's $V_{\rm max}$
restricted to its environment, the void$-$wall offset of $\feRealEnvVoidOffset$~dex exceeds the
two nulls by \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma\ and \feRealShufVoidEnvSigma$\sigma$, and the group$-$field
offset of $+\feRealEnvGroupOffset$~dex by \feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma\ and
\feRealShufGroupEnvSigma$\sigma$, the first against a null whose random groups hold a tenth of the
real members. The group excess lies in the most massive members, those with a group neighbour
within the FAST beam, which sit nearer their group centres; without them the offset is
$+\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvClean$~dex. Their line widths match those of isolated galaxies of the
same HI mass, which does not decide between blending and mass segregation. We claim neither
offset as environmental.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Void galaxies are expected to have a lower HI mass function (HIMF) knee than wall galaxies, and
group galaxies to lose gas to stripping \citep{solanes2001,jones2018}; \citet{moorman2014}, with an
estimator that does not assume uniform density, found the ALFALFA void knee $\sim$0.14~dex lower.
The FAST All Sky HI Survey \citep[FASHI;][]{fashi_dr1,fashi_dr2} now provides \feRealNDRTwo\
sources with a completeness $C$ and accessible volume $V_{\rm max}$ for each; the one environment
study on FASHI data used 230 group galaxies \citep{fashi_groups}. Environment splits of a
flux-limited $1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMF also select in distance, sensitivity and structure, so we
compare them with two nulls that fail in different ways, and test blending in FAST's beam. An
earlier version of this analysis, on DR1 with a single flux-limit weighting, reported a void offset
of $\feRealDROneMaxVoidKneeOffset$~dex without a null test.

\section{Data and Method}
\label{sec:data}
We keep the sources of Table~2 of \citet{fashi_dr2} with $C\geq\feRealCMin$, the sample of the
survey's own HIMF; weights $1/(C_i V_i)$ reproduce the published global HIMF
($\log M^*=\feRealGlobalLogMStar$, $\alpha=\feRealGlobalAlpha$, against 9.89 and $-1.31$).
Schechter fits are formally poor (reduced $\chi^2$ up to \feRealGlobalRedChi); their errors are
scaled accordingly. We use the \feRealBlendNPool\ sources inside both a padded box around the
Douglass+2023 VoidFinder voids \citep{douglass2023} and the SDSS footprint (1$^\circ$ cells
holding Tempel+2017 galaxies; requiring at least \feRealStrictMinPerCell\ per cell changes no
offset by more than \feRealStrictMaxShift~dex). Void members lie inside any void hole and walls are the rest;
group members lie within the projected $R_{200}$ and 1000~km\,s$^{-1}$ of a Tempel+2017 group
\citep{tempel2017}. We weight each galaxy by $C_i V_i\,g_E(d_{\max,i})$, where $g_E(D)$ is the
share of the volume within $D$ occupied by its environment $E$, from $2\times10^6$ randoms
classified by the same rules; the survey-wide weighting gives the same conclusions, with offsets
of $\feRealVoidKneeOffset$ and $+\feRealGroupKneeOffset$~dex.

The first null moves each void rigidly to a random position with all its holes in the footprint,
and each group to a random position with its velocity and $R_{200}$. The second gives the
environment label to the real number of members in each cell of redshift ($\Delta z=0.0025$) and
per-source detection sensitivity (quintiles of the catalogue's \code{rms\_beam}), drawn at random
(\feRealShufReps\ shuffles); terciles give the same result. A source is flagged when two or more
SDSS galaxies ($r<17.77$), normally one of them its counterpart, lie within 2.9~arcmin (the beam
FWHM used as a radius) and $W_{50}/2+100$~km\,s$^{-1}$. For line widths we instead use a fixed
$\pm300$~km\,s$^{-1}$ window, since a window that grows with $W_{50}$ selects broad sources, and
compare, within cells of HI mass and redshift, sources with a neighbour inside their flux
aperture plus half a beam, sources with a neighbour only in a 3~arcmin ring beyond it, and
isolated sources. Each excess over a null is divided by the measured offset's fit error and the
null spread in quadrature.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
The restricted offsets are $\feRealEnvVoidOffset\pm\feRealEnvVoidErr$ (void) and
$+\feRealEnvGroupOffset\pm\feRealEnvGroupErr$~dex (group). They exceed the placement nulls by
$\feRealEnvNullExcess$ and $+\feRealEnvGroupNullExcess$~dex, and the shuffle nulls by
$\feRealShufVoidEnvExcess$ and $+\feRealShufGroupEnvExcess$~dex (Figure~\ref{fig:nulls}). The
random void placements hold \feRealNullCNInVoidMin--\feRealNullCNInVoidMax\ galaxies against
\feRealNInVoid\ in the real voids; their offsets correlate weakly with occupancy (correlation
$\feRealEnvNullCorrOccEnv$), emptier placements giving less negative offsets, and they contain real
void members at \feRealNullCOverlap\% against \feRealVoidBaseRatePct\% of the sample, so the
placement excess is the conservative end for voids. The random group regions hold a median
\feRealGroupNullNInGroup\ members at $z=\feRealGroupNullMedZ$, against \feRealGroupNInGroupReal\ at
\feRealGroupMedZReal, so that null is weak by construction and sets no conservative end.

Of the group members, \feRealBlendGroupWfiftyFlagIn\% are flagged, against
\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyFlagOut\% of field galaxies; for \feRealBlendGroupSameGroupPct\% the
neighbour is in the same group, and flagged members lie at a median $R/R_{200}$ of
\feRealBlendGroupRRFlag\ against \feRealBlendGroupRRClean. They are also the most massive (median
$\log M=\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyLogMFlag$ against \feRealBlendGroupWfiftyLogMClean). Without them
the group offset is $+\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvClean\pm\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyEnvCleanErr$~dex
(\feRealBlendGroupWfiftyShufSigma$\sigma$ over the shuffles), a drop that removing the most massive
members would cause whatever its origin. At fixed HI mass and redshift, group members with a
neighbour inside their aperture have $\log W_{50}$ differing from isolated members by
$\feRealBlendGroupLwInInnerDiff\pm\feRealBlendGroupLwInInnerSe$~dex, and those with one only in
the ring by $\feRealBlendGroupLwInRingDiff\pm\feRealBlendGroupLwInRingSe$~dex.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
The void excess is \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma--\feRealShufVoidEnvSigma$\sigma$ depending on the null,
and we do not report it as a detection. A void is emptier than a typical randomly placed volume, so
no rigid placement matches its occupancy, and the shuffle that does has no spatial structure.

The group excess is positive, whereas stripping would lower the knee. Two explanations fit the
sign: blending, which adds a neighbour's HI to a source in a crowded core, and the concentration of
massive galaxies towards group centres. Because a blend's HI mass and line width grow together,
equal widths at fixed mass do not separate them, and the placement null is too weak to help; we
therefore do not attribute the group offset to environment. Interferometric HI for the flagged
members would decide it. For environment-split $1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMFs from single-dish surveys we
recommend both nulls, reported with their occupancy, and confusion flags built with a window that
does not depend on the line width.
