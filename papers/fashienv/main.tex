\title{Environment-Split HI Mass Functions in FASHI DR2 and Random-Placement Null Tests}
\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
We split the HI mass function of FASHI DR2 by environment and test the knee offsets against the
same splits made with voids and groups placed at random. With the survey-wide $1/(C\,V_{\rm max})$
weighting the void$-$wall and group$-$field offsets are $\feRealVoidKneeOffset$ and
$+\feRealGroupKneeOffset$~dex, and random placements alone give $\feRealNullCMean$ and
$+\feRealGroupNullMean$~dex. Restricting each galaxy's $V_{\rm max}$ to its own environment's
volume moves the null means to $\feRealEnvNullMean$ and $\feRealEnvGroupNullMean$~dex. The void
residual is nearly the same under both weightings ($\feRealNullCExcess$ and
$\feRealEnvNullExcess$~dex, \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma$\sigma$). The group residual grows from
$+\feRealGroupNullExcess$ to $+\feRealEnvGroupNullExcess$~dex (\feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma$\sigma$),
because the restriction moves the group null about twice as far as the measurement. Neither null is
density-matched, and we attribute neither offset to environment. Environment-split
$1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMFs need random-placement nulls, reported under both weightings.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Void galaxies are expected to have a lower HI mass function (HIMF) knee than wall galaxies, and
group galaxies to lose gas to stripping \citep{solanes2001,jones2018}; \citet{moorman2014}, with an
estimator that does not assume uniform density, found the ALFALFA void knee $\sim$0.14~dex lower. The FAST All Sky HI Survey
\citep[FASHI;][]{fashi_dr1,fashi_dr2} now provides \feRealNDRTwo\ sources, and its catalogue
supplies a completeness $C$ and accessible volume $V_{\rm max}$ for each; the one environment
study on FASHI data used 230 group galaxies \citep{fashi_groups}. Weighting environment
subsamples by that survey-wide volume assumes that each galaxy could have sat anywhere in the
survey, which an environment member cannot. We test splits under both this and an
environment-restricted $V_{\rm max}$ against random placements. An earlier version of this analysis, on DR1 with a single flux-limit weighting, reported a void
offset of $\feRealDROneMaxVoidKneeOffset$~dex without any null test.

\section{Data and Method}
\label{sec:data}
We take Table~2 of \citet{fashi_dr2} from the survey's public share and keep the \feN\ sources with
$C\geq\feRealCMin$, the sample of the survey's own HIMF. Weights $1/(C_i V_i)$ reproduce the
published global HIMF ($\log M^*=\feRealGlobalLogMStar$, $\alpha=\feRealGlobalAlpha$, against
$9.89$ and $-1.31$). A single flux-limit weighting on the same sample fails that check
($\alpha=\feRealOptASameGlobalAlpha$) and moves the void offset by
$\feRealWeightEffect\pm\feRealWeightEffectErr$~dex, so we do not use it. Schechter fits are formally
poor (reduced $\chi^2$ up to \feRealGlobalRedChi); their errors are scaled accordingly.

Void members lie inside any hole of a Douglass+2023 VoidFinder void \citep{douglass2023}; wall
galaxies are the rest of a padded bounding box around the voids (\feRealWallPct\% of the sample).
Group members lie within the projected $R_{200}$ and 1000~km\,s$^{-1}$ of a Tempel+2017 group
\citep{tempel2017}, in the heliocentric frame. For the environment-restricted $V_{\rm max}$ we
draw $2\times10^6$ randoms uniform in volume over the DR2 footprint, classify them with the same
rules, and weight each galaxy by $C_i V_i\,g_E(d_{\max,i})$, where $g_E(D)$ is the fraction of the
volume within $D$ that environment $E$ occupies and $d_{\max}$ follows from $V_i=(\Omega/3)\,d_{\max}^3$
over the survey area. Rescaling each $d_{\max}$ from the survey's distance scale to the randoms' $h=0.70$ scale (median
ratio \feRealEnvFrameRatio) changes the offsets by at most \feRealEnvFrameMaxShift~dex. The void share of
the classifiable volume rises from \feRealEnvVoidShareFirst\% within 50~Mpc to
\feRealEnvVoidShareAll\% overall; the group share falls from \feRealEnvGroupShellFirst\% within
50~Mpc to \feRealEnvGroupShellLast\% beyond 200~Mpc, as expected of a flux-limited group catalogue.

In the null each void is moved rigidly, with a random orientation, to a random position where all
its holes lie inside the SDSS footprint and the box; placed voids may overlap. Groups move to random
footprint positions with their velocities and $R_{200}$.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
With the survey-wide weighting the void$-$wall offset is
$\feRealVoidKneeOffset\pm\feRealVoidKneeErr$~dex (void-jackknife error \feRealJkErr) and the
group$-$field offset $+\feRealGroupKneeOffset\pm\feRealGroupKneeErr$~dex. The random placements
give $\feRealNullCMean\pm\feRealNullCStd$ (\feRealNullCReps\ void placements) and
$+\feRealGroupNullMean\pm\feRealGroupNullStd$ (\feRealGroupNullReps\ group placements; mean and
standard deviation), comparable to the published void effect; the residuals are
$\feRealNullCExcess$ and $+\feRealGroupNullExcess$~dex, and \feRealGroupNullNGe\ group placements
reach the measured group offset.

With the environment-restricted weighting the offsets are
$\feRealEnvVoidOffset\pm\feRealEnvVoidErr$ and $+\feRealEnvGroupOffset\pm\feRealEnvGroupErr$~dex.
The first \feRealEnvNullReps\ of the same seeded placements give $\feRealEnvNullMean\pm\feRealEnvNullStd$
and $\feRealEnvGroupNullMean\pm\feRealEnvGroupNullStd$~dex (Figure~\ref{fig:nulls}). For groups
the restriction moves the null mean by $\feRealEnvGroupNullShift$~dex but the measurement by only
$\feRealEnvGroupMeasShift$~dex, and \feRealEnvGroupNullNGe\ placements now reach the measured
offset. Both residuals are divided by the measured offset's fit error and the null spread in quadrature,
because the null reuses one galaxy sample.
The void residual, $\feRealEnvNullExcess$~dex, is \feRealEnvVoidQuadSigma$\sigma$ (no placement
reaches it); the group residual, $+\feRealEnvGroupNullExcess$~dex, is
\feRealEnvGroupQuadSigma$\sigma$ (rank $p\approx\feRealEnvGroupNullP$, or \feRealEnvGroupNullPTwo\
for the two environments tested).

Neither null is density-matched. The random void placements hold
\feRealNullCNInVoidMin--\feRealNullCNInVoidMax\ galaxies against \feRealNInVoid\ in the real voids,
and the random group regions a median \feRealGroupNullNInGroup\ against \feRealGroupNInGroupReal.
Across the paired void placements the offset correlates weakly with occupancy (correlation
$\feRealEnvNullCorrOccEnv$ under the restricted weighting, $\feRealEnvNullCorrOccSurvey$ under the
survey-wide one), emptier placements giving less negative offsets; the real voids lie outside the
sampled range, so we do not extrapolate. The group placements' occupancy, redshift and overlap
with real groups are collinear, and none of them sets a direction.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Restricting $V_{\rm max}$ to each environment removes
the part of the null shift that comes from the environment's share of the volume changing with
distance; what remains combines the estimator's response to the placed volumes with real structure
at those distances, and the null cannot separate the two. After subtracting the null, the void
residual barely depends on the weighting, while the group residual almost triples; a group signal
that appears under only one weighting is not yet a measurement.

We attribute neither offset to environment. The placed group regions hold a tenth as many
galaxies as the real groups. Blending of neighbours in FAST's $\sim$3~arcmin beam grows with
density, so it would raise the group knee and lower the void knee relative to the wall, the signs
seen; it is untested, as is the match between FASHI's $v_{\rm opt}$ frame and the void catalogue's
redshifts. Environment-split $1/V_{\rm max}$ HIMFs should be tested against random-placement nulls
under both the survey-wide and the environment-restricted volumes, with the null's occupancy
reported; for the group offset here, density-matched nulls and a blending test come next.
