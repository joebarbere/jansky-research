\title{Long-Period Transients Are Caught in a Few Per Cent of ASKAP Snapshots, and the
Published Ephemerides Rarely Say Why}

\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
Forced Stokes-V photometry at \ldNSources\ long-period transient (LPT) positions across
\ldNEpochs\ archival ASKAP/VAST snapshots constrains the per-snapshot probability of catching
a pulse whose circularly polarized flux clears threshold. Three sources with
detections give $p = \ldPMin$--$\ldPMax$, a range consistent with one common rate
($p_{\rm LRT} = \ldCommonRateP$); the valid limits span \ldLimMin--\ldLimMax\ at an assumed
5~mJy Stokes-V pulse. Dividing each $p$ by that source's own $(w+T)/P$ gives the implied
active fraction, \ldFaDetMin--\ldFaDetMax: the class is not uniformly ``mostly off''. The
split into active fraction and in-period duty cycle needs a pulse-anchored reference epoch:
\ldNNoEpoch\ of \ldNSources\ discovery papers publish none, and the one available split gives
$f_{\rm active} = \ldFActive$ ($<\ldFActiveUpper$ at 95\%), conditional on the published
epoch being a pulse arrival.
\end{abstract}

\section{}

Long-period transients pulse on minute-to-hour periods and are often silent: GPM
J1839$-$10 nulls for 50--70\% of its cycles \citep{hurleywalker2023}, and the accreting
white-dwarf binary ASKAP J174508.9$-$505149 ``turns off for several hours at a time''
\citep{rose2026}. These statements are per-source, from heterogeneous campaigns; the field
review notes that survey selection functions ``require explicit modeling before population
analysis is possible'' \citep{rea2026}. We measure a uniform per-snapshot quantity from
archival data alone, for the \ldNSources\ of 16 catalogued LPTs with VAST coverage in the
committed epoch table.

A snapshot of length $T$ on a source of period $P$ whose emitting phase has width $w$ catches
a pulse with probability $p = f_{\rm active}\,(w+T)/P$ when the source is active. Detection
counts constrain the product. Using forced Stokes-V photometry at the catalogued positions in
\ldNEpochs\ VAST snapshots, we count a detection where $|V|/\sigma_V \ge 5$ and $|V|$ exceeds
the on-axis leakage floor, matching \citet{barbere2026lptv}; $p$ is therefore a Stokes-V
threshold quantity, and converting it to total intensity requires the circular fraction,
which spans tens of per cent across the class.

The denominator is the summed per-epoch detection efficiency $\Phi(S/\sigma_V - 5)$ rather
than the epoch count, a principled choice whose measured effect here is small: at the
observed pulse brightnesses it removes \ldEffRemovedHeadlinePct\% of the nominal exposure
(more at fainter assumed fluxes, where it matters). Efficiencies below $10^{-3}$ are floored
to zero so shallow epochs cannot sum into sensitivity that does not exist. Upper limits are
exact one-sided 95\% Poisson values, capped at 1.

Three sources yield detections, $p = \ldPMin$--$\ldPMax$; a likelihood-ratio test finds the
three rates indistinguishable from one common value ($p = \ldCommonRateP$). The physical
scale comes from dividing each $p$ by its own $(w+T)/P$: the implied active fractions are
\ldFaDetMin\ (ASKAP J1832$-$0911), 0.061 (ASKAP J175534.9$-$252749.1, matching the
phase-resolved \ldFActive\ below), and \ldFaDetMax\ (ASKAP J183950.5$-$075635, whose narrow
$(w+T)/P$ makes its modest $p$ imply a source active in most cycles). Non-detections give
$p < \ldLimMin$--$\ldLimMax$ at an assumed 5~mJy Stokes-V pulse; the same division shows
the numerically tightest $p$-limit is not the most constraining: the strongest implied
bound is $f_{\rm active} < \ldFaLimBest$ (GLEAM-X J162759.5, whose long snapshots cover
two-thirds of a cycle), and \ldNFaLimVacuous\ limit excludes nothing at all. All limits are
tabulated against assumed flux in the committed evidence.

The binomial treatment assumes snapshots sample pulse phase uniformly and independently; a
uniformity test needs no ephemeris, since clustering is invariant under a phase shift. Of
\ldNSources\ sources, \ldNClustered\ shows non-uniform sampling (Rayleigh $Z = \ldClusterZ$,
Bonferroni-corrected over sources and tests $p = \ldClusterP$) and its constraint is void;
\ldNInconclusive\ has a period quoted too coarsely to test. \ldNValid\ constraints stand:
\ldNValidUncond\ unconditionally, since for \ldNPdotCond\ sources the published $\dot P$
bound, if saturated, would smear phase past coherence. Uniformity is also not
independence: if activity persists across a cycle, the Poisson limits weaken by up to
$\sim$55\%; the point estimates are unbiased either way.

Separating the factors needs a pulse-anchored epoch, and the published record is the limit:
\ldNNoEpoch\ of \ldNSources\ discovery papers tabulate no reference epoch (GPM J1839$-$10
knows its period to $2\times10^{-4}$~s and publishes no PEPOCH), and one more
\citep{pritchard2026} publishes an epoch with a period too coarse to use. Of
\ldNPublishedPepoch\ published PEPOCHs, ASKAP J175534.9$-$252749.1 \citep{mcsweeney2025}
admits the split: \ldFActiveOn\ of \ldFActiveN\ snapshots overlap its pulse window and one
contains a detection, giving $f_{\rm active} = \ldFActive$ ($<\ldFActiveUpper$ at 95\%),
conditional on the PEPOCH being a pulse arrival, for which the only evidence is that
detection's own phase (a-priori landing chance \ldFActiveApriori). ASKAP J1832$-$0911's
detection falls at phase \ldJEDPhase\ of the \citet{wang2025} epoch, and its $\dot P$ bound
allows \ldJEDSmear\ cycles of smear at the farthest epoch, so its split is unavailable.
ASKAP J183950.5$-$075635 \citep{lee2025} folds on the anchor of \citet{barbere2026lptv}; the
pulse and interpulse phases (\ldPhaseA, \ldPhaseB) are a consistency check against that
companion analysis (same anchor and period), not an independent validation.

The measurement is limited by ephemerides for most of the sample (a published,
pulse-anchored epoch would convert several of these products into bounds on the activity
fraction), but not for all of it: for ASKAP J183950.5 and J165130.3, whose snapshots cover
a few per cent of a cycle, the epoch count, not the ephemeris, is the limit.
