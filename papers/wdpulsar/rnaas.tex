\title{No Persistent Radio Emission from the Pelisoli et al.\ White-Dwarf-Pulsar
Candidates: a Forced-Photometry RACS Census}

\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
Forced Stokes-I$+$V photometry at every RACS-covered position from the \wdNCand\
AR\,Sco-like candidate list of \citet{pelisoli2025}, in every public RACS epoch, yields zero detections in either
Stokes parameter across the \wdRealNCandCovered\ covered candidates (median $3\sigma$
circular-polarization limit \wdRealMedianVLimit~mJy), bounding the persistently
radio-loud fraction at $f < 6.1\%$ (95\%). The confirmed white-dwarf pulsar in the
list, J1912$-$4410, is itself undetected, consistent with its low duty cycle; the
bound therefore constrains persistent emission only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result}

The white-dwarf pulsars AR\,Sco \citep{marsh2016} and J1912$-$4410 \citep{pelisoli2023}
emit pulsed, variable, circularly polarized radio emission; \citet{pelisoli2025}
selected \wdNCand\ further AR\,Sco-like binary candidates from Gaia$+$WISE, but their
follow-up was optical and X-ray, and no systematic radio search of the list existed.
We perform one from the archives: forced Stokes-I and Stokes-V photometry at every
candidate position in every public Rapid ASKAP Continuum Survey \citep[RACS;][]
{mcconnell2020} low- and mid-band observation (887 and 1367~MHz), with the measurement
locked to the catalogue position so a non-detection is an honest limit. A Stokes-V
detection must clear $5\sigma$ (an instrumental-leakage veto is also applied but never
binds at these fluxes); limits are the deepest $3\sigma$ single epoch per target,
i.e.\ a best-case snapshot depth. Of \wdRealNAttempted\ attempted epoch measurements,
\wdRealNUsed\ were usable.

The census is a clean null. Across the \wdRealNCandCovered\ RACS-covered candidates:
\wdRealNCandIDet\ Stokes-I detections above $5\sigma$, \wdRealNCandVDet\
circularly polarized detections, to a median $3\sigma$ V limit of
\wdRealMedianVLimit~mJy (range \wdRealVLimMin--\wdRealVLimMax~mJy; Figure~\ref{fig:wd} shows their
distribution; the machine-readable per-candidate table,
\code{results/wdpulsar\_table.json}, is in the archived repository,
\citealt{janskyresearch}). With zero detections among \wdRealNCovered\ covered
candidates the 95\% Poisson bound on the persistently radio-loud fraction of
the photometrically selected population is $f < 2.996/\wdRealNCovered = 6.1\%$ above
the per-target limits; the bound's sensitivity is calibrated by the control's
measured 2-of-5 per-epoch detection efficiency below.

The template AR\,Sco (deliberately not among
the \wdNCand) is re-detected in Stokes~I in 2 of its 5 usable epochs (both
RACS-mid, best $I = \wdRealControlI$~mJy at \wdRealControlSnr$\sigma$; undetected in
both RACS-low epochs, 1.1$\sigma$ and 1.7$\sigma$) and never reaches the census's
$5\sigma$ bar in circular polarization (best epoch $3.4\sigma$). The V threshold is
therefore conservative even for the archetype, and the measured per-epoch I efficiency
entering the $f$ bound is 2/5.
The second calibration is J1912$-$4410, the one confirmed white-dwarf pulsar in the
list, undetected in
all \wdRealJEpochs\ of its epochs ($3\sigma$: $I < \wdRealJLimI$,
$V < \wdRealJLimV$~mJy): pulsing at $\sim$15~mJy for $<$4~s at a $\sim$1\% duty cycle
\citep{pelisoli2023}, it time-averages to $\sim$0.1--0.2~mJy, below these limits. A
snapshot non-detection is therefore fully consistent with a genuine, active white-dwarf
pulsar. The census bounds only the persistent, high-duty-cycle minority; targeted
time-resolved follow-up (the route that confirmed J1912$-$4410) remains the discovery
path for the rest. The seven candidates outside the RACS footprint are unconstrained
here; one of them, the previously uncharacterised J0408$+$6046, has a 1.25~mJy VLASS
3~GHz counterpart consistent with its young-stellar classification rather than an
AR\,Sco-like signature (see the archived full analysis).
