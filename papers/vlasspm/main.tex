\title{No Optically Dark Compact Radio Movers in Four VLASS Epochs}
\author[0009-0008-3289-4447]{Joseph Barbere}
\affiliation{Independent researcher}

\begin{abstract}
We searched three VLASS Quick Look epochs over the whole survey, and a fourth over
\vpmRealAreaFour~deg$^2$, for moving radio sources, consulting optical and infrared catalogues only at the end. The search recovers the unresolved UV/BL~Ceti binary at
$\vpmRealUVFitMu\pm\vpmRealUVFitErr$~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$ (three epochs, Gaia parallax; system
motion \vpmRealSysMu) and finds no other mover. With errors calibrated on static sources, a
compactness cut calibrated on nearby Gaia stars leaves UV~Ceti the only one of
\vpmRealNCandBefore\ candidates; the rest are static in the images. For compact sources of
\vpmRealFluxLoThree--\vpmRealFluxHiThree~mJy moving at \vpmRealRateMid--\vpmRealRateMax~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$
with no co-moving Gaia or CatWISE counterpart, the 95\% upper limit is $\vpmRealLimThree$~deg$^{-2}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Search}
\label{sec:search}
Radio proper motions from survey data have so far been measured for stars selected by their Gaia
proper motions \citep{driessen2023,de2024}, and \citet{atri2022} measured VLBA proper motions of
compact variable radio sources without optical counterparts, a targeted follow-up rather than a
blind search. A blind search could find movers with no optical counterpart, such as cool brown
dwarfs or fast pulsars. The VLA Sky Survey \citep[VLASS;][]{lacy2020} has covered the sky
north of Dec $-40^\circ$ three times at 3~GHz with a 2.5-arcsec beam, and a fourth epoch covers
\vpmRealAreaFour~deg$^2$ so far. We used the CIRADA component tables for epochs 1 and 2
\citep{gordon2021} and the NRAO Quick Look lists for epochs 3 and 4, all fitted by PyBDSF
\citep{pybdsf}: after the quality cuts they hold \vpmRealNEOne, \vpmRealNETwo, \vpmRealNEThree\
and \vpmRealNEFour\ components observed between \vpmRealTStart\ and \vpmRealTEnd.

A component with no counterpart within 2.5~arcsec in another epoch is an orphan, and only
orphans with no other component within 30~arcsec in their own epoch are used, because split
extended sources produce correlated orphans. Orphans in two epochs are linked when the implied
rate lies between \vpmRealRateMin\ and \vpmRealRateMax~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$ and the shift is
significant at $3\sigma$; a third epoch must then contain an orphan within $3\sigma$ of the
extrapolated straight line at its own observing date. Every triple of epochs is searched.

Each component's positional error is the elliptical Gaussian error of \citet{condon1997}, with
the effective signal-to-noise of \citet{condon1998} and the exponents as in \citet{prandoni2000},
computed from its fitted shape, beam and peak signal-to-noise (it reproduces the catalogue
errors). To it we add an astrometric floor measured from bright static sources in
declination bands (\vpmRealFloorMin--\vpmRealFloorMax~arcsec) and a structure term: a resolved
source's centroid moves between epochs by a fraction \vpmRealKStruct\ of its deconvolved size
along each of its axes. That fraction was fitted to the offsets of \vpmRealNStaticPairs~million
pairs of isolated static sources so that their $3\sigma$ tail matches a Rayleigh distribution;
on a held-out half of the sky the fraction beyond $3\sigma$ falls from \vpmRealTailMeas\ with the
catalogue errors to \vpmRealTailFit\ (expected 0.011). The errors remain too narrow beyond about
$4\sigma$ (\vpmRealTailFitFive\ of static sources beyond $5\sigma$) and south of Dec $-35^\circ$
(\vpmRealTailSouthRatio\ times the Rayleigh fraction beyond $3\sigma$).

The candidates of an earlier version of the search were almost all resolved static sources, and
this led us to add a compactness cut (deconvolved major axis over beam major axis). Its threshold
and rule were then fixed on injections before it was applied to the data. Faint point sources are
not fitted with zero size, so injected sizes were drawn, by signal-to-noise, from \vpmRealNStars\ detections of stars within
100~pc in the Gaia Catalogue of Nearby Stars \citep{gcns2021} (about \vpmRealNStarChance\
expected by chance). The criterion was the smallest threshold keeping at least 95\% of the
recovered injections overall and in every signal-to-noise bin, with the cross-epoch rule chosen
by its rejection of the general static population. Two of three detections below \vpmRealCutThr\
keep \vpmRealKeepAll\ of the injections (\vpmRealKeepLowSNR\ below signal-to-noise 7) and remove
\vpmRealStaticRej\% of static sources; requiring all three needs \vpmRealCutThrAll\ and removes
\vpmRealStaticRejAll\%.

Rotating the later epochs by a few arcminutes in right ascension measures chance alignments of
unrelated orphans; it cannot see a static source whose fitted centroid shifts between epochs.
\vpmRealNInj\ movers with log-uniform rates, injected at random sky positions with the same
errors and size noise, give the completeness. On a synthetic field the pipeline recovers
\vpmSynNRecovered\ of \vpmSynNMovers\ planted movers with \vpmSynNFalse\ false candidates; all
\vpmSynNMissed\ missed movers are slower than \vpmSynMissedMuMax~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$, where the static
match radius removes them.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
The mover found in epochs 2--4 at the position of UV~Ceti is the unresolved Luyten 726-8 system:
UV and BL~Cet are \vpmRealCompSep~arcsec apart and both have Gaia RUWE of
\vpmRealRuweMin--\vpmRealRuweMax. A straight line through the three detections, weighted by their
covariances and with the Gaia DR3 parallax (\vpmRealUVGaiaPlx~mas) fixed, gives
$\vpmRealUVFitMu\pm\vpmRealUVFitErr$~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$ ($\chi^2=\vpmRealUVFitChi$ for 2 degrees of
freedom), and \vpmRealUVNoPlxMu\ without parallax. The system moves at \vpmRealSysMu\ (UCAC4,
\citealt{ucac4}, via SIMBAD, \citealt{simbad}), \vpmRealSysSigma$\sigma$ away; Gaia DR3
\citep{gaiadr3} gives \vpmRealUVGaiaMu\ for UV~Cet and \vpmRealBLMu\ for BL~Cet. Parallax, orbital
motion and blending of the two components can each account for differences of this size. Its
compactness is \vpmRealUVCompA, \vpmRealUVCompB\ and \vpmRealUVCompC, passing on two detections.

Before the compactness cut the search returns \vpmRealNCandBefore\ candidates. Cutouts of the
other \vpmRealNStaticVetted\ show emission persisting at the first-epoch position, so they are
static; \vpmRealNSouthVetted\ lie south of Dec $-35^\circ$, where the
beam in some epochs is strongly elongated. The scrambled catalogues predict at most \vpmRealNullCand\ chance candidates per trial. The cut removes all \vpmRealNStaticVetted,
two of them narrowly: \vpmRealMarginAName\ and \vpmRealMarginBName\ would pass at thresholds of
\vpmRealMarginAThr\ and \vpmRealMarginBThr. No by-eye step therefore lies in the chain from the
catalogues to the limit. A candidate is optically dark if no Gaia DR3 or CatWISE2020
\citep{catwise2020} source is co-moving, that is, within $3\sigma$ (plus 0.5~arcsec) of the
track's position at the catalogue epoch or with a proper motion within 30\% of the radio rate.
After the cut this step has only UV~Ceti to classify, so chance coincidence cost nothing here;
at \vpmRealNChance\ random footprint positions a source lies within 5~arcsec with probability
\vpmRealChanceFive, and within the co-moving rule's \vpmRealComovingRadius~arcsec with
\vpmRealChanceComoving.

Completeness is near zero below \vpmRealRateLo~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$, where a mover stays within the
static match radius, and rises across \vpmRealFineEdgeLo--\vpmRealFineEdgeHi~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$
(\vpmRealFineCompA\ at \vpmRealFineEdgeLo--\vpmRealFineEdgeMid, \vpmRealFineCompB\ at
\vpmRealFineEdgeMid--\vpmRealFineEdgeHi, at least \vpmRealFineCompAboveMin\ above;
Figure~\ref{fig:comp}). The mean over the three log bins from \vpmRealRateMid\ to
\vpmRealRateMax~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$, a log-uniform rate weighting, is \vpmRealCompThreeCut\ at
\vpmRealFluxThree~mJy (\vpmRealCompThreeNoCut\ without the cut) and \vpmRealCompOneFiveCut\ at
\vpmRealFluxOneFive~mJy (\vpmRealCompOneFiveNoCut). With no new mover, the 95\% upper limit over
the \vpmRealAreaDeg~deg$^2$ searched in three epochs is $\vpmRealLimThree$~deg$^{-2}$ for sources
of \vpmRealFluxLoThree--\vpmRealFluxHiThree~mJy per epoch and $\vpmRealLimOneFive$~deg$^{-2}$ for
\vpmRealFluxLoOneFive--\vpmRealFluxHiOneFive~mJy, or fewer than \vpmRealAllSky\ over the whole sky
if movers are isotropic. The worst rate bin gives $\vpmRealLimWorstBin$, and movers just above
\vpmRealFineEdgeLo~arcsec~yr$^{-1}$ $\vpmRealLimEdge$~deg$^{-2}$. The heavy error tails can
overstate the completeness by at most \vpmRealTailBoundThree\% at \vpmRealFluxThree~mJy and
\vpmRealTailBoundOneFive\% at \vpmRealFluxOneFive~mJy. The injections move on straight lines, so
the completeness does not apply within \vpmRealPlxDistMin~pc, where parallax moves the third-epoch
point by more than one standard error (\vpmRealUVPlxResid~arcsec for UV~Ceti). The limit applies
only to movers unresolved at 2.5~arcsec: the cut removes a mover with resolved emission, such as
a pulsar with a bow-shock nebula, and a binary a few tenths of a beam wide fitted as one elongated
source in two epochs.
